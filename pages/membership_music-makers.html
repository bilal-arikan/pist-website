Inside the music studios

Studios feature high-end equipment like Neumann condenser mics, SSL audio interfaces, PMC studio monitors, hardware synths, instruments, and more from the industry’s top brands. Learn more.

Studios are designed by industry-leading acousticians and feature bespoke acoustic treatment and concrete shells for a tonally neutral recording environment & premium sound.

Get into your flow state faster with simple, plug & play systems that have everything you need to create. No more wasting time with complicated setups or lugging heavy equipment with you. Just bring your laptop with you.

Each studio strikes the perfect balance between luxury and comfort, from mood lighting and pleasant scents to Herman Miller chairs, cosy sofas, AC, and more.
