## 3. Why we process it

- To read and respond to your enquiry
- To hold pre-contractual discussions where relevant
- To meet legal obligations and to have evidence in case of a dispute

Your data is **not used for marketing**, not added to a mailing list, and not sold.

## 4. Legal basis

| Purpose | Basis under KVKK |
|---|---|
| Responding to you | Art. 5/2-c — directly related to conclusion or performance of a contract |
| Keeping a record | Art. 5/2-f — legitimate interest |
| Legal obligations, disputes | Art. 5/2-ç and 5/2-e |

## 5. How long we keep it

- Enquiries answered and closed: **2 years**
- Discussions that become a working relationship: **10 years** after it ends
  (Turkish Code of Obligations limitation period)

After that, data is deleted, destroyed or anonymised.

## 6. Your rights

Under KVKK Art. 11 you may ask us to confirm whether we process your data, tell you
how and why, name the third parties it was transferred to in Türkiye or abroad,
correct it if inaccurate, delete or destroy it, notify those third parties of any
correction or deletion, object to decisions made solely by automated analysis that
affect you adversely, and compensate you for damage caused by unlawful processing.
